# Source and method notes

This proposal is derived only from the frozen local target catalogue, Stage 1C.2 polygon overlay v001 and Stage 1C.2.3 boundary-distance audit v001. No new earthquake queries or supporting event downloads were made.

Hasterok subplate labels are used as exact candidate child-unit tags. PB2002 plate and orogen names, Hasterok geological provinces, and nearest-boundary classifications remain separate evidence fields. The event-level proposal does not assign fault causation, sequence membership, final study-region membership, or control status.

The seven original geometric overlap events and all 640 outside-pilot events remain in the event-level file.
