## Proposed next paper

Working title:
"Beyond Single-Parameter Earthquake Precursors: A Confounder-Aware, Multi-Domain Machine Learning Framework for Testing Lithosphere–Atmosphere–Ionosphere Coupling"

Potential contribution:
- A predeclared multi-domain benchmark.
- Global, algorithmically selected earthquake catalogue subject to documented data-coverage eligibility.
- Domain-specific background models and quality-aware anomaly scores.
- Matched spatial controls and surrogate tests.
- Comparison of multimodal fusion with seismicity-only and environmental baselines.
- Event-blocked, chronological and geographic holdouts.
- Transparent reproducibility package.

Avoid claiming "first in the world" until a systematic prior-art search has been completed. Avoid claims of operational prediction.
